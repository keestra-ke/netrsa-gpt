# Keja Scan feature roadmap

Four phases. Phase 1 is what this repo ships now (web + Android APK). Later phases wait on a real backend, Maps key, and M-Pesa.

## Phase 1 — Marketplace MVP (now)

**Build:** tenant / landlord / agent accounts · post vacancy with photos, estate pin, features, contact method · verified phone+ID badge (demo OTP `1234`) · search, feed, Mtaa View · 30-day auto-expiry · vacancy counter · landlord dashboard (views, inquiries) · Android APK.

**Revenue later:** featured listing KSh 500/week · Pro KSh 3,000/month. The checkbox is in the form; money is not collected yet.

**Viral now:** share a listing URL · feed of new rooms · “post a vacancy” on the home screen.

## Phase 2 — Search intelligence

Comparisons · rent-by-estate charts · saved searches / SMS alerts · filters (generator, furnished, water).

**Revenue:** paid search placement · area insight reports.

## Phase 3 — Estate services

Movers, vibarua, water, door breaker booked from a listing · student / campus filters · short videos · matatu commute time.

**Revenue:** 5–10% booking commission.

## Phase 4 — Trust + payments

Neighbourhood overlays · landlord/tenant reviews · M-Pesa rent · deposit escrow · land/new developments.

Do **not** skip Phase 1 supply: 20 real rooms from 5 caretakers beat every Zillow-style chart.
